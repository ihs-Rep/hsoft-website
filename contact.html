Contact & Support

Have feedback, discovered a bug, or want to suggest a new utility? We'd love to hear from you.

Direct Channel Notice: HSoft is an independent software project currently preparing for its official public release. Direct email addresses and GitHub repository links will be activated alongside the first official tool release. In the meantime, you can review the contact channels and inquiry formats below.

Inquiry Categories

Please select an appropriate subject when contacting us to ensure a fast response:

🐛 Bug Reports

Encountered an issue with one of our utilities? Please include your Windows edition (e.g., Windows 11 23H2), tool version, and steps to reproduce.

💡 Feature Requests

Have an idea for a new feature or a new standalone Windows tool? We welcome practical suggestions that improve user workflows.

💬 General Questions

Inquiries regarding software licensing, compatibility, and future release schedules.

Configuration Note for Site Owner

To configure your actual contact email, open contact.html and update the form action or replace the placeholder with your domain mailbox (e.g. contact@hsoft.lk).

Send a Message

Fill out the form below. For static deployment, submissions can be routed to Formspree, Cloudflare Workers, or your preferred mail service.
