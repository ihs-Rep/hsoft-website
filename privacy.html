Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Review our commitments to transparent, privacy-respecting software and website operations.

1. Introduction

At IHSByte, we prioritize user transparency and respect the privacy of individuals visiting our website and utilizing our Windows utility software. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is handled, and your rights concerning your personal information.

2. Website Information & Hosting

This website is hosted statically via Cloudflare Pages. When you browse the website, standard web server logs and network-level metadata (such as your IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, and request timestamp) may be processed by Cloudflare's edge infrastructure to ensure network security, DDoS mitigation, and caching.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

The IHSByte website does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. Any client-side state (such as dark/light theme preference) is stored strictly on your local device via standard browser localStorage and is never transmitted to an external server.

4. Contact & Correspondence Data

If you contact IHSByte directly via email at contact@ihsbyte.com.lk for bug reports, support queries, or feature requests, we receive the information you choose to provide (such as your name, email address, Windows build details, and problem description).

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and troubleshoot reported software issues. We do not sell, rent, or trade your correspondence data.

5. Windows Utility Software & Zero Silent Telemetry

Our core development philosophy is that utility applications should operate natively and privately on your Windows computer:

  • Offline Functionality: Unless explicitly noted (such as network socket diagnostics), IHSByte utilities operate entirely offline.
  • Zero Silent Telemetry: IHSByte desktop applications do not collect or transmit personal files, keystrokes, browsing history, or background activity.
  • Crash Logs & Error Reporting: In the event of a crash, no diagnostic logs are uploaded unless you explicitly inspect and transmit them as part of a voluntary bug report.

6. Third-Party Download Providers

Software binaries and installers for IHSByte utilities are distributed through trusted release platforms such as GitHub Releases. When downloading an installer, your request is handled directly by GitHub's infrastructure and is governed by GitHub's Terms of Service and Privacy Statement.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our software practices, please contact us directly at contact@ihsbyte.com.lk or review our Contact Page.

IHSByte develops lightweight, high-performance Windows utilities and delivers independent tech research and cybersecurity analysis.
