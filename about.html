About HSoft

Independent software development focused on practical, high-utility Windows tools and desktop solutions.

Our Mission

HSoft develops focused, practical software utilities designed to solve tangible everyday computing problems. Our tagline — "Small Tools. Smart Solutions." — reflects our dedication to creating compact, single-purpose Windows applications that deliver high impact without background bloat, unnecessary subscriptions, or intrusive telemetry.

Modern desktop computing is frequently burdened by heavyweight software suites, complex multi-step configurations, and forced cloud integrations. HSoft builds desktop-native software that does exactly what it promises, executes instantly, and respects user autonomy.

Core Solution Areas

HSoft develops software utilities across four primary operational domains:

1. System Security, Privacy & Access Control

Utilities like Adobe Blocker and Decoy provide kernel-level hosts filtering, ultra-fast Windows Firewall batch injection, and tamper-proof administrative controls. They empower users to restrict unwanted background telemetry, prevent unauthorized bypass, and enforce distraction-free desktop environments.

2. Business Data Protection & Automated Backups

Critical data protection shouldn't require complex enterprise appliances. Applications such as MailSafe offer automated, scheduled redundancy for Outlook email archives (PST/OST files), accounting databases, and essential office directories to local disks, external drives, and mapped network storage.

3. Desktop Productivity & Workflow

Lightweight productivity tools like FocusPoint streamline daily task management and recurring schedules with zero-latency floating capture windows, unobtrusive system notifications, and minimal RAM footprint.

4. Network Management & IT Diagnostics

Developed for system administrators and multi-user environments, utilities like Net Mode SMB Monitor enable instant network adapter isolation (switching between local QuickBooks networks and internet access) while delivering real-time throughput metrics and audit tracking for SMB file servers.

Who We Build For

  • IT Support Professionals & System Administrators: Engineers requiring lightweight, portable tools for network diagnostics, firewall control, and workstation policy enforcement.
  • Office & Accounting Workstations: Teams running Outlook, file servers, or multi-user software that demand dependable local backups and isolated local network modes.
  • Developers, Power Users & Creators: Users who demand clean native executables without background spyware, promotional adware, or telemetry overhead.
  • Home & Family Users: Anyone seeking straightforward, dependable utilities for Windows maintenance and secure web filtering.

Our Development Standards

  • 100% Standalone & Native: We prioritize native Windows APIs, C#, WPF, and PowerShell integration for maximum performance and minimal system overhead.
  • Zero Silent Telemetry: Our desktop utilities operate locally on your PC. No personal documents, keystrokes, or network activity are ever collected or sent to remote servers.
  • No Bundled Adware: We never bundle third-party toolbars, browser hijackers, or unwanted software wrappers. Every release includes verified SHA-256 checksums.
  • Transparent Changelogs: We maintain honest, detailed documentation, verified compatibility tables, and direct support channels.
Official Inquiries & Support: For feature suggestions, bug reports, or enterprise feedback, contact us directly at info@hsoft.lk or visit our Contact Page.
